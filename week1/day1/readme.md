 *Salesforce Summer Program - Day 1
 *What is CRM?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system used by companies to manage customer data, communication, sales, and support in one place

## Why Companies Use Salesforce

- To manage customer information
- To track sales and leads
- To improve customer relationships
- To automate business processes
- To increase productivity

## Salesforce Concepts

### Account
An Account represents a company or organization.

Example:
- TCS
- Infosys
- Amazon

### Contact
A Contact represents a person associated with an account.

Example:
- Employee
- Customer
- Manager

### Opportunity
An Opportunity represents a potential business deal or sale.

Example:
- Software purchase
- Service agreement

---

## Business Flow

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

Explanation:

1. Lead:
A person shows interest in a product or service.

2. Contact:
The lead details are stored as a contact.

3. Opportunity:
Business deal or sales discussion starts.

4. Customer:
After successful purchase, the person becomes a customer.

---

## Real World Mapping (College Admission)

- Account = College
- Contact = Student
- Lead = Student applying for admission
- Opportunity = Admission process
