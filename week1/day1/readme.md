Salesforce Summer Program - Day 1

What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

CRM helps companies store customer details and manage communication, sales, and support in one place. It helps businesses understand customers better and improve their workflow.

What I understood about Salesforce

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used by companies to manage customers, sales, and business activities.

Why Companies Use Salesforce

- To store customer information
- To track leads and sales
- To improve customer communication
- To manage business workflow
- To automate tasks and save time

Salesforce Concepts

Account

Account means a company or organization.

Examples:
- TCS
- Infosys
- Amazon

Contact

Contact means a person related to a company or account.

Examples:
- Employee
- Customer
- Manager

Opportunity

Opportunity means a possible business deal or sale.

Examples:
- Product purchase
- Service agreement

Business Flow

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

Lead:
A person shows interest in a product or service.

Contact:
The person's information is stored in the system.

Opportunity:
Business discussion or sales process starts.

Customer:
After successful purchase, the person becomes a customer.

Real World Mapping (College Admission)

Account = College

Contact = Student

Lead = Student applying for admission

Opportunity = Admission process
